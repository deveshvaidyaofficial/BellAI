A friendly voice for everyday reminders

Your loved one’s day, gently remembered. BellAI phones at the right moment to remind them about medicines, appointments and the small tasks that are easy to forget.

One call, one small thing. Easy to answer, easy to trust.

No app to learn and no buttons to press. BellAI uses the telephone your parent already knows how to use.

  1. Tell us the routine

    A family member adds medicines, doctor visits and daily tasks with the times they matter.

  2. BellAI calls on time

    A warm, unhurried voice reminds them, answers questions and waits for a reply.

  3. They answer in their own words

    “Yes, I’ve taken it” or “Not yet” is enough. BellAI follows up if the task is still waiting.

It sounds like a kind neighbour, not a machine.

Conversations are natural and patient. People can ask BellAI to repeat itself, say what they need, or tell it they’ve finished, and it understands.

Call to Maya, 8:00 am

Good morning, Maya. It’s time for your blood pressure tablet. Have you had breakfast?

Yes, I just finished. Which one is it again?

The small white one, from the box marked morning. Let me know when you’ve taken it.

Done. Thank you, dear.

Marked as taken. Her daughter has been told.

The things that are easy to forget, handled.

Anything that repeats, or only happens once, can become a call.

  • MedicinesThe right tablet at the right time, with a check that it was taken.
  • Medical appointmentsA reminder the day before and again on the morning of the visit.
  • Daily essentialsDrinking water, a short walk, locking the door, a meal.
  • Questions at any time“What time is my appointment?” gets a clear, calm answer.

Independence for them. Peace of mind for you.

Your parent stays in charge of their own day at home. You see which reminders were confirmed and which were missed, so you can step in only when it counts.
